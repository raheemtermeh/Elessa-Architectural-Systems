## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for an elite architectural engineering brand specializing in automated entryways, glass railings, smart facades, and electric roll-up systems. The brand communicates precision engineering, transparency, modern luxury, and safety. 

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Architects, commercial developers, interior project managers, and upscale residential homeowners seeking premium architectural hardware and entrance solutions.
- **Emotional Response:** Confidence in German-grade reliability, sleek technological advancement, architectural fluidity, and effortless minimalism.

### Design Style: Modern Architectural Glassmorphism
The aesthetic synthesizes crisp, light-flooded architectural photography with luminous emerald-tinted accents. Subtle glassmorphic layers echo the actual glass handrails and automatic sliding doors manufactured by the company. Fluid rounded geometry, generous whitespace, pristine charcoal typography, and glowing mint green call-to-actions deliver an approachable, futuristic, and distinctly executive RTL digital experience.

## Colors

The color system pairs architectural purity with high-tech vitality:

- **Primary (`#10B981`) & Neon Mint (`#00E676`):** Represent energy, dynamic automation, and precision. Applied to high-impact conversion buttons, status tags, hover highlights, and radiant drop glows.
- **Secondary / Deep Slate (`#0F172A`):** The primary color for bold typography, high-contrast structural dark modules, and header status bars, offering grounded architectural stability.
- **Surface Background (`#FAFCFB`) & Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Create an airy, daylight-infused showroom atmosphere reminiscent of pristine structural glass and polished aluminum.
- **Glass & Translucency:** Used across floating navigation headers, sticky quotation bars, and technical specification cards to evoke the product lineâ€™s structural glass materials.

## Typography

The design system incorporates a modern, geometric sans-serif aesthetic specifically tailored for Persian right-to-left layout dynamics. In production, fonts map to contemporary Persian geometric typefaces such as **Vazirmatn** or **Dana**, with `Plus Jakarta Sans` functioning as the universal geometric companion for Western numerals and Latin code models.

### Typographic Rules:
- **Persian Glyphs and Numerals:** Numerical values within technical dimensions (e.g., mm, kg, motor torque ratings) must support Persian localized numbering alongside Latin technical units.
- **Line Heights:** Given the taller ascenders and descenders of Persian script, line heights are increased by 10â€“15% compared to standard Latin baselines to prevent glyph clipping.
